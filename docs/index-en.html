Short answers

Can I buy a single module?

Yes. Take KDV, Trial Balance or SGK on its own; the others stay installed but locked until their own licence. Adding a module later unlocks the same installation, no second download.

How does buying work?

Buying runs through a person: download the module for free, contact us (WhatsApp or e-mail), and your product key (licence) is assigned to your device from the server. On request we give a live demo over screen sharing. No credit card.

Does the trial balance module replace Luca?

No. Luca stays your accounting program; this module pulls data from Luca, compares the trial balance with the ledger detail and prepares the report. Your Luca licence is separate.

I bought all three; what if the computer changes?

The licence is tied to the device. Install on the new computer and write to us; the authorisation moves and the old device is released.
