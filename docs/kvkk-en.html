What stays on your computer

Your invoices, ledger records, control tables and every generated report are processed and stored only on your own computer. They are never sent to the server. There is no screen recording or hidden collector running in the background; the only optional data feature is the anonymous usage statistics described below, and nothing is sent unless you turn it on.

Optional anonymous usage statistics

The switch on the application's Settings screen is off by default. While it is off, no usage data is collected or sent; the feature only runs with your explicit consent.

When you turn it on, the application periodically sends the following to the licence server (Cloudflare):

  • Event type and date: how many times you opened the application, ran an audit, downloaded a report, checked for an update or hit a crash, and on which days.
  • Numeric counters: values such as audit duration and the number of errors, warnings and processed lines.
  • Application version and a random device ID generated at first install. It is not linked to your hardware or identity; it only serves to count distinct devices.

This data contains no text fields at all: company names, tax numbers, client names and file names cannot be sent — the software is written so that only numbers and predefined short codes can leave your machine. On the server, data is kept as counters aggregated per day, event type and version, not per customer. Crash reports themselves stay on your computer; only the fact that a crash occurred is counted. Turning the switch off stops all further transmission.

Collection method and legal basis

The technical data above is collected by the automatic HTTPS request the application makes at startup. The legal basis is the performance of the license contract and our legitimate interest (preventing license abuse).

Transfer abroad (Article 9, Law No. 6698)

The data is stored on the infrastructure of Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers & KV), our hosting provider. Cloudflare servers sit outside Türkiye, in various regions of the world, so the device fingerprint, IP address and technical records are transferred abroad.

RecipientCountryData transferredPurpose
Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers, KV) United States and global edge locations Device fingerprint, IP address, operating system, application version; if the statistics switch is on, anonymous numeric counters and a random device ID Licence verification and record keeping
GitHub, Inc. United States IP address (only during the update check) Checking whether a new version exists
Google LLC (Gmail) United States Your e-mail address and the contents of the correspondence Only if you send us an e-mail

The storage location cannot be chosen, so the transfer is inseparable from the service. For that reason the application asks for your explicit consent to the transfer abroad on first launch; without consent it does not open. Your consent is stored, with its date and text version, on your own computer in %USERPROFILE%\.kdv_kontrol\kvkk_onay.json. You may withdraw it at any time by writing to info@muhasebepaketi.com.tr.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) only to count visits. It writes a first-party cookie named _ga to your browser; the IP address is anonymised and the data is processed on Google's servers. There are no advertising, profiling or third-party tracking cookies; fonts and icons are served from our own domain, so no other CDN request is made while you visit. You can delete or block the analytics cookie in your browser settings; the site keeps working. The application collects no usage data unless you turn on the statistics switch described above.

Retention period

  • Licence record: while the licence is valid and for 2 years afterwards.
  • Server operation logs: at most the last 1,000 records; older records are deleted automatically.
  • E-mail correspondence: 2 years after the correspondence ends.
  • Data on your own computer: until you delete it.

To have your record deleted, write to info@muhasebepaketi.com.tr with your device fingerprint (shown in the application); the record is then deleted. Note that once the licence record is gone the application can no longer verify itself and locks.

Data breach notification

If a security breach affecting your personal data is detected:

  • The breach is notified to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board within 72 hours of our becoming aware of it.
  • Affected people are informed directly (by e-mail) as soon as reasonably possible.
  • The notification states the nature of the breach, the categories of data affected, the possible consequences and the measures taken.

Your rights (Article 11, Turkish Data Protection Law No. 6698)

  • Learn whether your personal data is being processed
  • Request information if it has been processed
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly
  • Know the third parties to whom data is transferred, in Turkey or abroad
  • Request correction of incomplete or incorrect processing
  • Request deletion or destruction of the data
  • Request that corrections and deletions be notified to the third parties involved
  • Object to a result produced solely by automated analysis that works against you
  • Claim compensation if unlawful processing causes you damage

You can send these requests to the e-mail address or phone number above.
